## 1. Purpose of This Project

DeTriz Smart Home Kit is a **learning project**, not a startup. The product concept (a plug-and-play IoT prototyping kit with a Live Studio dashboard and a pre-wired design system) is a realistic vehicle for practice, but success is measured by **what I learn**, not by revenue, users, or market fit.

The goal is to go through **the whole product-building process, from scratch to release**, and practice every product area along the way.

### Two core learning goals

1. **Go deeper into the technical side** of building real products: code, testing, APIs, CI/CD, Git, frameworks, developer tools, and even electronics fundamentals.
2. **Make design-to-code handoff smoother** by building both sides myself and learning where things get lost in translation.
3. **Bring AI into my everyday workflow** as a practical tool for designing, coding, and learning faster.

---

## 2. Learning Assumptions

| # | Assumption | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Not a business** | No business goals, monetization, pricing, or growth metrics. Decisions are made based on learning value, not commercial value. |
| 2 | **Problem space is given** | We skip problem discovery and validation. The problem described in the PRD is treated as already defined — the focus is on the **solution and delivery** space. |
| 3 | **Practice the full lifecycle** | Every phase (design → code → test → deploy) is touched at least once, even if the result is small. Breadth of process over depth of features. |
| 4 | **Start small, end-to-end** | The first deliverable is a single, simple component taken all the way to a deployed product — not a half-built large system. |
| 5 | **AI as a learning tool first** | In Phase 1, AI is used to **explain, teach, and review** — I write and understand the code myself. |
| 6 | **AI as a builder second** | In Phase 2, AI agents do most of the building, so I can compare speed, quality, and my level of understanding against Phase 1. |
